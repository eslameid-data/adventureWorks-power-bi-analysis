# Adventure Works Sales & Profitability — Business Insights

All figures below are based on the final validated Power BI model and the `AdventureWorks Sales.xlsx` dataset. The analysis covers transactions from July 2017 through FY2020. The Date table extends to June 2021, but no sales transactions exist after FY2020.

## Headline Numbers

- **Total Sales:** $110,336,782
- **Total Cost:** $97,257,908
- **Gross Profit:** $13,078,874
- **Gross Margin:** 11.85%
- **Total Orders:** 31,455
- **Total Retail Customers:** 18,484
- **Active Reseller Partners:** 636

## 1. Revenue Growth

Sales increased significantly across the three fiscal years represented in the transaction data:

| Fiscal Year | Sales | YoY Growth |
|---|---:|---:|
| FY2018 | $24.0M | — |
| FY2019 | $34.1M | +42% |
| FY2020 | $52.2M | +53% |

Revenue more than doubled from FY2018 to FY2020, with growth accelerating in FY2020.

**Business Insight:** The company shows a strong upward revenue trajectory, with FY2020 representing the strongest sales year in the dataset.

## 2. Product Concentration — Bikes Dominate

Product category revenue is heavily concentrated in Bikes:

- **Bikes:** 86.2% of total revenue — approximately $95.1M
- **Components:** 10.7%
- **Clothing:** 1.9%
- **Accessories:** 1.2%

Bikes also generate approximately **$11.0M of gross profit**, representing roughly 84% of total gross profit.

**Business Insight:** Bikes are the primary revenue and profit engine, but this creates significant category concentration risk. A substantial decline in Bikes demand could materially affect overall business performance.

## 3. Channel Mix — Reseller-Led Business

Revenue is primarily generated through the Reseller channel:

- **Reseller:** 73.4% of revenue — approximately $81.0M
- **Internet:** 26.6% of revenue — approximately $29.4M

The Reseller channel generated approximately **3,796 orders through 636 active partners**, while the Internet channel generated approximately **27,659 orders across 18,484 retail customers**.

**Business Insight:** Although the Internet channel contains far more individual customers and orders, the Reseller channel is responsible for the majority of revenue. This indicates a strongly reseller-driven business model.

## 4. Customer Concentration

Customer revenue within the Internet channel is highly concentrated:

- Median customer lifetime spend: approximately **$270**
- 75th percentile: approximately **$2,511**
- **Top 20% of Internet customers generate 66.4% of Internet-channel revenue**

**Business Insight:** A relatively small portion of retail customers contributes a disproportionately large share of Internet revenue. Identifying, retaining, and growing these high-value customers can have a significant impact on revenue performance.

## 5. Geographic Performance

The United States is the dominant geographic market:

| Country | Sales |
|---|---:|
| United States | $63.3M |
| Canada | $16.4M |
| Australia | $10.7M |
| United Kingdom | $7.7M |
| France | $7.3M |
| Germany | $4.9M |

The United States alone contributes approximately **57% of total sales**.

The United States and Canada together account for approximately **72% of total revenue**, while the United Kingdom, France, and Germany collectively represent approximately **18%**.

**Business Insight:** Revenue is strongly concentrated in North America, while European markets represent a meaningful secondary opportunity with lower individual-country contribution.

## 6. Profitability by Category

Bikes generate the largest absolute gross profit because of their dominant revenue volume.

However, absolute gross profit and gross margin answer different business questions:

- **Gross Profit** measures the actual dollar contribution generated by a category.
- **Gross Margin %** measures profitability relative to the category's sales.

This distinction is important when comparing categories with very different revenue sizes.

**Business Insight:** Bikes dominate absolute profitability, while category-level margin analysis provides additional insight into how efficiently each category converts revenue into gross profit.

## 7. City-Level Gross Margin Observation

A city-level analysis revealed an important analytical consideration.

The highest five cities by Gross Margin % showed an identical margin of approximately **62.6%**. These cities had extremely small sales volumes, with only a few transactions each.

The identical margin is explained by the underlying product-cost structure and the very small transaction volumes rather than evidence that these cities are genuinely the company's most profitable markets.

**Business Insight:** Margin percentage should not be used alone to rank geographic markets. Gross Profit, Sales volume, and transaction count should be considered together to distinguish meaningful performance from small-sample effects.

## 8. Customer Sales — "Not Applicable" Category

Customer-level sales analysis shows a very large **"Not Applicable"** customer grouping compared with individual named customers.

This occurs because Reseller transactions do not have an associated retail `CustomerKey`. Those transactions therefore appear under a non-applicable customer grouping rather than being assigned to individual retail customers.

The result is consistent with the channel structure of the dataset: Reseller sales represent approximately **73.4% of total revenue**.

**Business Insight:** Customer-level analysis should distinguish between **retail customers** and **reseller partners**. Combining the two into a single customer ranking can create misleading results because reseller revenue is not associated with individual retail customer records.

## 9. Opportunities

### European Market Development

Europe contributes a smaller share of total revenue than North America, while the United Kingdom, France, and Germany already demonstrate established sales activity.

**Opportunity:** Further analysis of European product, customer, and reseller performance could identify markets with growth potential.

### Reseller Productivity

A relatively small group of active reseller partners generates the majority of company revenue.

**Opportunity:** Measuring revenue, gross profit, and average sales per reseller could identify high-performing partners and reveal opportunities to improve underperforming accounts.

### Retail Customer Development

The top 20% of Internet customers generate 66.4% of Internet revenue.

**Opportunity:** Customer retention and targeted development of high-value retail customers could provide a meaningful contribution to Internet-channel growth.

## 10. Key Business Risks

### Revenue Concentration

Bikes generate approximately **86% of total revenue**.

**Risk:** Heavy dependence on a single product category increases exposure to changes in Bikes demand.

### Reseller Dependency

The Reseller channel generates approximately **73% of total revenue**.

**Risk:** Revenue concentration among reseller partners means the loss or deterioration of several major partners could materially affect overall revenue.

### Geographic Concentration

North America accounts for approximately **72% of total revenue**.

**Risk:** Heavy dependence on a limited number of geographic markets increases exposure to regional market changes.

### Gross Margin

Overall Gross Margin is approximately **11.85%** based on product cost.

**Risk:** This is a gross margin measure and does not include operating expenses. Therefore, it should not be interpreted as bottom-line or net profitability.
